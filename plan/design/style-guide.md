# Style guide: Mylanchi

## Canvas
- 1080 × 1920 px, RGB, one artboard per frame (`01-names` … `08-reply`)
- Safe area: no text within 150 px of the top or 250 px of the bottom
- Background: the palm photo, warm and softly vignetted. Frame 7 uses ivory card.

## Color
| Role | Hex |
|---|---|
| Palm light | `#EDCBA8` |
| Palm deep | `#D9A47A` |
| Wet henna paste | `#3B271B` |
| Henna stain | `#A2451A` |
| Deep stain | `#6F2812` |
| Ivory card | `#F7EFE3` |
| Text on palm | `#4A1D0F` |

## Type
| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| Names | Pinyon Script |
| Story lines, details | Cormorant Garamond (italic for story lines) |
| Arabic | Amiri |
| Malayalam | Noto Serif Malayalam |

## Rules
- Text always sits in clear skin, never on top of the henna
- Story lines sit at the top, names and details at the bottom
- Raised paste look: add a tiny Drop Shadow (1 px, 35%) on wet-paste layers
- Stain look: Multiply blend, no shadow

## Export
- JPG quality 11, sRGB, under 1 MB each: `01-names.jpg` … `08-reply.jpg` in `exports/frames/`
- Video: 3–4 s per frame, 30 fps, MP4 H.264. Soft music, and a gentle sound on the frame 6 colour change.
