01 The envelope

Shoot a real airmail envelope flat on a wooden table, or build the striped border on its own layer.

It slides onto the desk from the top edge and lands with a small bounce.

بِسْمِ ٱللَّٰهِ ٱلرَّحْمَٰنِ ٱلرَّحِيمِ
Dear you,
Before we share the news,
let us tell you a story.
It begins with two people
who had never met.
Two towns, two families,
both praying for the
same good thing…
J & R

02 The letter

Blue aerogramme paper with a fold line. Write it by hand in real fountain pen and scan it; that beats any font.

The letter unfolds, then the words write themselves in.

Once upon a time, in Kundoor…

childhood photo
JUNAID RAZIQ

03 His stamp

A childhood photo turned into a postage stamp. Duotone it in the stamp colour (Gradient Map) and mask the perforated edge.

The postmark thumps down onto the stamp.

…and in Mattan, a girl named Rafeeda.

childhood photo
RAFEEDA

04 Her stamp

The same template, mirrored, in a second colour. Put her birth year (or any year) in the postmark.

This stamp slides in from the opposite side.

Then, one day, news travelled fast.

05 The telegram

An old telegram form with white typed strips pasted on at slight angles. Each strip goes on its own layer.

The strips appear one at a time with a typewriter clack.

There's one more thing inside.

☪J R

open it with a bismillah

06 The wax seal

Order a real J R wax seal stamp (cheap online) and photograph it. That one prop makes the whole set look premium.

The seal cracks and the flap swings open.

08 The reply card

A reply-paid postcard showing how to RSVP. On WhatsApp, guests can send a photo of it ticked.

A pen ticks the first box.

These are layout mockups, drawn in code. In Photoshop, swap each piece for real photographed paper, stamps and a real wax seal, and keep the positions and text. The parts you still need to fill in are how you met (it could be a second telegram strip) and the WhatsApp number on the reply card.
