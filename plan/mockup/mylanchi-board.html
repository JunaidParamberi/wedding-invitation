01 The hidden names

A full mandala on a palm. "Junaid" and "Rafeeda" are woven into the second ring in tiny script. Guests zoom in to find them.

The mandala draws itself outward from the centre, ring by ring.

Once upon a time, in Kundoor…

Junaid Raziq
son of Mohammed Ali

02 His home

A big paisley with a Kerala house and a coconut palm drawn inside it. A vine trails out to the right, towards the next frame.

The paisley outline draws first, then the house appears inside it.

…and in Mattan,

Rafeeda
daughter of Abdul Kareem

03 Her home

The mirror of frame 2: the paisley faces the other way, with her house and a crescent moon inside. The vine enters from the left.

This one draws in mirrored, from the other side.

Two homes.
Two families.

Slowly, their paths grew toward each other.

04 The vine

A henna vine with leaves winds down the palm from one small house to the other. This is where your how-we-met line goes.

The vine grows along its path, and the leaves unfurl as it passes.

Then both families said yes.

ٱلْحَمْدُ لِلَّٰهِ
Alhamdulillah

05 Two become one

The two paisleys from frames 2 and 3 meet and curve into a heart, under a crescent and star.

The two paisleys slide in from each side and lock together.

They say the darker the mylanchi, the deeper the love.
